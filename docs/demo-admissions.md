# Admissions information - fictional demo data

## Application period
Applications open on June 1 and close on August 30. Submit your application through the admissions portal.

## Tuition and scholarships
Tuition for Information Technology is 18 million VND per semester. Eligible students may receive a scholarship covering up to 50 percent of tuition.

## Required documents
Applicants provide the application form, high-school transcript, graduation certificate and identification. Admissions support is available Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 5 PM.
